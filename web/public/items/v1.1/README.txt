TORN VEIL 360 / V2

Separate iteration. Original v1 remains unchanged. Stable TV-001–TV-300 IDs retain their physical role; 60 new TV-301–TV-360 arcane-technology designs introduce six mechanical systems. V2 refines all Uncommon and higher originals with functional component detail. Normal items intentionally retain practical v1 variants. Upper tiers adopt founder-set lore, stronger assemblies and 3/4/5 ability specifications.

ASSET SCOPE
360 static GLBs, metric gallery master, twelve independently usable 30-item Blender packs. Searchable embedded-image visual HTML and JSON. All named materials are PBR base materials, without texture artwork or UV layout. No gameplay code, collision or LODs. Parts may intersect; not watertight manufactured solids.

FITTING RIGS
160 male/female fitted armor variants (80 armor IDs). Tall generic 1.90 m mannequin, approximately 8.5-head convention. 18-bone generic skeleton, not a supplied production skeleton. Static pauldron/bracer/greave studies remain left-side pieces; fitted variants include mirrored complementary right-side mates. Helmet binds to head; shoulder to upper arm; bracer to forearm; greave to shin. Cuirass upper shell binds rigidly to chest; overlapping lower lamellae bind to spine. V2.1 replaces the fitted shoulder cups with arm-axis-aligned separate lames, distal external pivot seats and open underarm clearance. All 32 paired male/female shoulder variants pass nine generic contact tests, including the original full raised-arm range and five intermediate angles, against matching helmet, cuirass, bracers and head/chest/upper-arm fitting envelopes. This does not certify a final game character or every possible animation. No breasts, genitalia or anatomical sculpt distraction. Materials and construction are preserved.

Four pose actions: neutral, walk-like stride, raised arms, crouch. These are stress-test motions, not finished locomotion. Every weighted vertex has one normalized influence. Neutral transforms and finite posed mesh coordinates are checked. Full character skin clearance, cloth behavior, gameplay retargeting and final game-skeleton compatibility are NOT certified. Other animations, cross-set combinations and final character skins still require production fitting. Generic envelope renders and numerical deformation checks do not prove final wearability. Production fitting requires the actual character and target skeleton.

EXPORTS
Static GLBs: Z-up Blender geometry exported as standard Y-up glTF. Individual static origins centered horizontally at ground level. Gallery master uses translations only. Fitted GLBs include generic skeleton and four NLA pose-test tracks. Fitted armor uses full character coordinate space, meters. Master fitting scene contains all variants; isolate one item and sex to review. Pose preview kit is Vesper Archive, IDs 345–349.

VALIDATION
See geometry-validation.json, rig-validation.json, export-validation.json and delivery-manifest.json. Before/after sheets use identical stable IDs with independently normalized display framing. This is a prototype collection, not a production-ready claim.
