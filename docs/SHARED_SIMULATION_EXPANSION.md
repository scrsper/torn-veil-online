# Shared simulation expansion — October 9, 2026

The user approved the larger shared-mechanics route after the October 8 audit. This
supersedes that audit's pending scope decision. Work starts from `654857a` on the
isolated `codex/connected-realms` checkout. The character worker retains ownership
of its separate mesh/rig candidate; that unapproved candidate is not installed here.

## First implemented stage: shared ability foundations

- The Tower ability catalogue now lives under `sim/physical/arcana`; the prototype
  re-exports the same definitions. Its run rewards and checkpoints remain scoped.
- A held canonical book or finite essence teaches an ability with a source event and
  item identity. Re-reading is idempotent. An ordinary scroll is consumed on accepted
  casting. Client ranks, power values and legacy Tower rewards are never imported.
- A body holds its reserve, cooldowns, accepted cast timeline and bounded effects.
  The ordinary scheduler resolves casts after windup against current positions and
  physical occlusion. Movement can cancel preparation without refunding costs.
- Damage uses `Simulation.applyHit`, ordinary conflict, injury, downing and explicit
  lethal intent. Healing cannot revive or remove localized injuries. Finite wards and
  authored physical armor absorb impact; equipment identity/condition survives saves.
- Body-bound bridge commands expose learning and casting. No new GUI is claimed in
  this first stage. Players and NPCs invoke the same canonical functions.
- Save schema 28 retains versions 24–27 and rejects malformed new components. New
  cast/source/effect state is validated against canonical source events on restore.

This is a foundation, **not complete Tower mechanic parity or a completed canonical
Tower replacement**. Element-specific burn/chain/pull behavior, persistent zones,
weapon imbues, source composition and higher-rank metaphysics still need further
implementation and acceptance. The original rich prototype remains available.

## Second implemented stage: presentation and contact

The shared presentation registry resolves existing body assets and phase-retimed
clips without changing canonical IDs or mechanical truth. The world adapter now
selects strikes/dodges, corrects asset facing, resumes locomotion after actions and
uses measured stride pacing. Physical recoil is persisted in schema 29 and collision
swept. NPC attack plans can guard/reposition from fresh sight of occupied approaches
and committed attacks. Realm movement now respects the same bodily limitations.

Actual before/after footage, hardware observations and the remaining visible quality
gaps are recorded in [the motion report](evidence/motion-oct9/RESULTS.md). This is
a bounded checkpoint; reference-quality movement and a canonical Tower replacement
remain unfinished. No new mesh candidate has been accepted by this implementation.

## Third implemented stage: reusable interior geometry

The existing Tower now imports its unchanged room/corridor grammar from canonical
code. A bounded folded-interior grid adds stable physical layouts and retained edits
without changing existing realm save indices. Legacy floor hashes, navigation,
collision and cache/reload tests cover this geometry boundary. It is not yet attached
to World saves or a new ascent route. See [the geometry contract](FOLDED_INTERIOR_GEOMETRY.md)
for the host, encounter and persistence work still required.

## Consequential progression constraint

The existing embodied Normal→Iron path still requires its meaningful practice,
foundations, knowledge, recovery and history across three world days. Realm saves
currently run at time scale 1. A four-to-six-hour climb cannot satisfy that history
gate just by relabeling floor progress. The new shared route must represent actual
metaphysical capability and evidence; it must not manufacture elapsed history.
Silver means regional strategic power and Gold means national power in the
Constitution. Neither label is granted by a floor counter in canonical state.

Four-to-six hours, summit normally Silver/near Gold, and one-to-five percent Gold
remain provisional design targets, not observed playtest results. Existing earned
canonical advancement remains intact while the expanded path is built.

## Motion and asset contract

The motion reference was inspected by the coordinator's analyst. Its useful observed
qualities are clear stance, whole-body anticipation/contact/recovery, grounded turns,
victim displacement and attackers managing space. It does not establish engine,
frame rate, IK or a specific blending algorithm.

Shared presentation must consume canonical timing and displacement. Replacing a
mesh, clothing kit or clip must not alter collision, reach, damage, inventory, action
windows or saves. Existing licensed clips and shared retargeting come first. Matched
actual gameplay captures, including five opponents, are required before a visible
quality improvement is claimed.

## Additional character idea

A persistent fictional female documentarian is feasible as a separately versioned
character identity plus an explicitly curated, public-safe change journal. Her special
knowledge must be source-grounded and isolated from ordinary NPC perception and
gameplay authority. The first useful slice is an in-world journal/camera hook and
private draft vlog package. Name/personality remain configurable. No consciousness
claim, public upload, account creation, external data sharing or automatic publishing
is authorized. This is design scope only until movement and shared combat are secure.
