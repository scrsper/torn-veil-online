# Living Economy & Survival

This milestone starts from main `31a9782`. It changes canonical provisioning, procurement, production, and food planning. The implementation passes the normal regression suite, but the primary survival target is **not met**: the final Ashford run has more residents at zero energy than the untouched-main baseline. One specialized adaptive-society acceptance check also fails. It does **not** add starvation death or permanent deprivation injury. Normal settlement support must be demonstrated before adding those consequences.

## Causes addressed

An untouched-main run of Ashford, seed 918271, at 0.15 physical seconds per step lost 964 silver in 30 world days. Wallets and household purses fell from 1,510 to 546; including the unchanged 40 coin items, total currency fell from 1,550 to 586. The tavern and hunter charged external supply costs while generating their stock without a material supplier. At day 30, 13 of 32 uncontrolled residents had zero energy. The baseline report is `.debug/economy/baseline-918271-30d.json`.

Other defects found during implementation were overlapping reservations against every perishable batch, procurement taking a supplier's entire load for partial payment, speculative delivery requests withholding retail food, repeated unfunded material orders, and grain processing stopping while its apparent output was already promised elsewhere. Movement also allowed a completed trip to stop just outside a small food counter's place bounds.

## Canonical mechanisms

- Household members bring surplus food home and contribute to or withdraw from an existing common purse while physically home. Membership and ownership grant access; merely entering somebody's house does not. A shopper keeps one meal, leaves the rest as household property, and preserves the food's age and spoilage pressure. Contributions are bounded; no household is guaranteed solvency.
- A locally observed pantry count can motivate the ordinary `provision_home` goal. Its plan buys real stock from a known vendor or picks up personally owned surplus available at the member's current location, then carries it home. The latter uses the existing two-meal portion action, keeps one meal, rechecks ownership at pickup, and excludes freight cargo. Producers can therefore provision dependants from actual output without first selling and buying it back. Pantry beliefs update on visits, rather than following remote inventories.
- Food purchasing compares actual local offers. Remembered price failures are distinguished from empty shelves, carry event provenance, influence source choice, and prevent an agent from repeatedly treating a known unaffordable meal as immediately achievable. Paid work can then compete through the existing goal system. Physiology still limits labor.
- Empty-shelf observations temporarily exclude that counter even when the person sees its sign again. If every known counter was empty, the person physically checks another counter and attempts a normal purchase. Searches prefer unknown services, then the least recently checked counter; the old nearest-shop/sign-only loop could miss replenished food indefinitely. Observed meal quotes strengthen the existing labor incentive when savings cannot cover the next meals; routine duties can give way to earning food money.
- Ordinary socializing now uses the same physiological constraint as social duties and purposes. Previously maximum sociability could outweigh maximum off-schedule hunger, even with money and a known food source. This changes competing motivation; it neither supplies food nor guarantees a successful search.
- Learned services and recent pantry, price, and input-failure observations have practical retention priority within the existing bounded knowledge map. Routine events about acquaintances previously displaced shop knowledge and even minutes-old failed purchases. Stale observations lose this priority; neither provenance nor confidence is upgraded by retention. A retry timer also no longer prevents investigating untried counters after several known shops were empty.
- Hunger can motivate production outside a routine shift, but only against an open order or the worker's own accepted order. An order claimed by somebody else cannot keep a hungry worker indefinitely trying to make a meal they cannot produce through the request system.
- Trying work after hearing about its shortage records new firsthand evidence. Only one's own previous attempt can be quietly reconfirmed. A detail learned through hearsay also retains that evidence's confidence when it refines an older, more certain claim; certainty about the old claim does not certify the new detail.
- A practiced food producer can answer witnessed empty counters by making food, or by hauling an input they have personally found missing. Delivering or witnessing new inputs retires that stale shortage belief. Supply concerns also reach sowing, harvesting, felling, and gathering through the existing shared motivation cap; a field shift uses real crop actions instead of competing with an inert generic work plan.
- Wholesale procurement pays the actual producer at pickup before title transfers. A buyer can fund fewer units, never take unpaid units. New orders budget materials and freight from the issuer's wallet. Retail stalls and stores buy their merchandise; revenue can move upstream through the mill, bakery, farms, and timber suppliers.
- Freight wages are paid for each leg actually delivered. A later failed leg neither erases useful completed labor nor pays for missing goods. Requests record actual payment; self-payment is not income. Operator-owned production pays workers through existing request mechanics.
- When a workplace loses its operator, living staff or an actual replacement recorded in a work stint can contract for new supplies and own new production. Existing land titles and old stock do not transfer. Procurement can buy a living producer's new batches even when older estate stock remains at the same place.
- Unclaimed orders express demand. Active carriers reserve outgoing stock once across batches; expired claims release it. Production can replace outgoing stock before it is collected. Stockpiles preserve distinct producers' ownership.
- Personal purchases cannot merge into employer-owned cargo. Purchases and freight preserve source age and accumulated spoilage. Delivery retains the ownership settled at pickup.
- Brewing transforms three local grain into six ale during work and requires a hot hearth. Fuel is consumed by the existing fire simulation. The tavern can procure both kindling and sustained log fuel. Cooks do not light a fire for a meat batch whose ingredients are missing.
- Hunting draws meat from finite local game capacity through the existing extraction action, with physical presence, capability, skill, work time, depletion, and gradual recovery. Authored and procedural worlds register these grounds; procedural capacity depends on local resources. Hunting is a coarse biomass model, not individual simulated animals.
- A hunter's scheduled forest work executes timed extraction. Physiology distinguishes walking to work, carrying cargo, quarrying, and tracking game from the action and body pose; merely intending a heavy job no longer charges its full exertion cost throughout the trip.
- Farmers harvest finite ripe plots even when current grain and bread stocks are high. The former 500-grain gate could leave most of a harvest to rot within six days although replacement crops take at least six weeks. Stored harvests now depend on real plots, yields, labor, seed consumption, and spoilage. The long-run regression bounds grain by those actual inputs, and the throughput diagnostic uses nutritional demand rather than the removed cap.
- Actual movement toward a haul endpoint renews its claim through a shared player/NPC helper. A 40-minute timeout previously reassigned healthy carriers' jobs during longer trips. Idle claims still expire; replacement acceptance updates the request and obligations, and an old claimant cannot act on the replacement's cargo. Resuming a loaded trip goes to the destination.
- Sleep now applies its already-defined fatigue recovery in the shared physiology step, alongside sleep-debt recovery. Previously, live sleep never called the fatigue recovery path: a procedural baker's fatigue could remain at 1 through successive nights and prevent further production despite food, flour, and a workplace. Caloric expenditure during sleep remains real.
- Navigation search effort scales with trip distance, with a 100,000-iteration ceiling. Procedural millers and cooks previously had valid roads to work that generation could find, while the NPC's fixed 9,000-iteration search falsely declared those commutes unreachable. The correction changes search effort, not walkability or movement speed.
- Navigation respects the requested destination height when approaching counters. Ashford's stall columns had walkable but inaccessible raised surfaces, so routes to their ground-level counters failed despite reachable adjacent ground. Failed or out-of-reach travel now stops dependent plan actions; it cannot invent an empty-shelf observation at a shop the person never reached.
- Player movement and NPC path following share a terrain-step constraint. NPC crowd separation previously could push a ground-level walker into a raised column, after which ground snapping lifted them onto its roof. In procedural site 3 this stranded the baker at day 12; bread stopped despite flour and money, and all 15 residents eventually reached zero energy. A focused reproduction confirmed the movement defect. Invalid crowd pushes now fall back to the valid path step; invalid path segments replan. That diagnostic collapse is not evidence of economic scarcity.
- Sleep and sit anchors obey the same terrain constraint and an unobstructed local approach. If furniture cannot actually be occupied, the body rests at its reached position; an animation anchor cannot teleport it onto a roof or through a wall.
- Crop projection refreshes the affected navigation column. Procedural field initialization could clear an obstruction while leaving navigation's cached cell blocked. A farmer then remained unable to leave despite food and savings; reloading the save, which rebuilt navigation, hid the defect. The focused cache regression and a fresh four-day replay verify that the farmer can reach water and return to work without a reload.
- Unreachable investigations and combat approaches use the existing pursuit cooldown. Aborting a failed journey cannot leave the agent retrying the same impossible pursuit indefinitely or falsely mark the investigation completed.

All of these mechanisms operate in `src/sim/`. Existing action, request, ownership, knowledge, event, and persistence systems remain authoritative. Save schema 20 intentionally rejects older worlds with incompatible economic and resource initialization.

The favor trace now distinguishes adopting a repayment errand from completing a material benefit. Its debt-closure assertion applies to witnessed completed requests, healing, gifts, returned property, and settlement events. An errand left unfinished by a shortage is reported as unfinished; the trace does not infer repayment from intention.

The responsibility trace raises a disclosed, funded order for existing stock sized against actual carrying capacity. The conflict trace stages three substantial gifts of existing property, then observes autonomous responses and ordinary work. These establish the conditions the scenarios are intended to exercise instead of selecting another lucky seed whenever ordinary schedules change. Existing persistence, priority, switching, and deferred-purpose checks remain intact.

The conflict observer now compares full-precision priorities and records the existing protected dwell window. It checks for unexplained priority reversals across the observed village; a protected ongoing purpose can legitimately hold its place over a more pressing alternative. The trace reports how many comparisons fell outside that window, including zero in the current run. A strengthened direct regression verifies that protection holds before expiry and that the more pressing purpose takes over afterward. No canonical priority rule was changed to satisfy the trace.

The food-scarcity stress fixture now removes standing crops as well as stored reserves. Leaving the ripe crop intact let real labor recover the original shock: a diagnostic run harvested 317 plots and completed 48 transformations within 1.5 days, without an attempted operation missing its inputs. The stronger crop-loss fixture retains its shortage, bounded physiology, and population-survival assertions and additionally requires zero harvests. This distinguishes successful recovery from an artificial requirement to fail.

## Measurement and limits

`npm run economy:trace -- --seed 918271 --days 30` produces a read-only JSON report with food, currency, household purses, actual payments, failed food access, physiological energy, game stocks, and a canonical hash. Add `--site 0` through `--site 3` to use an existing procedural settlement specification. There is no diagnostic feedback into agents or production.

The model still has coarse nutrition, short brewing batches rather than fermentation inventories, fixed wage and price conventions, no credit, and no complete fiscal or service-income model for civic and religious occupations. Inheritance of existing estates, storage capacity, and continuing demand for some crafts also remain incomplete. Grain, fuel, labor, savings, and food can run out. Sharing cannot support a household with no incoming resources indefinitely. Different histories are permitted; matching every settlement to a target survival rate is not an invariant. Conversely, these remaining distribution and income gaps mean that observed deprivation cannot all be called a demonstrated systemic collapse.

The three implementation runs use the same final runtime, including the terrain, rest-anchor, and crop-navigation corrections. Zero energy means the caloric reserve floor, **not death**. Payments are cumulative circulation, so the same coin can appear in several transactions. Failed attempts are events, not distinct people. These figures demonstrate functioning trade, paid labor, and household provisioning; they do not demonstrate adequate purchasing power for every household.

Ashford's outcome is worse than baseline: 25/32 residents at zero energy versus 13/32. Its count rises from 7 at day 12 to 13 at day 20 and 25 at day 30, while 23 of the final 25 have less than two silver. Bandit wallets hold 448 silver, up from 90 initially. Robbery is one real route by which money concentrates, but these measurements do not isolate its contribution from income, distribution, production, and food-access limitations. Conserving the currency removed an artificial sink; it did not resolve affordability.

The remaining failure includes productive occupations: all six Ashford farmers, the hunter, and the woodcutter finish at zero energy. The farmers together hold only 3.77 silver, while the two bakers hold 522.25, the two merchants 232.14, and the miller 141. The world still contains 2,309 grain and 49 flour. This rules out treating missing civic wages or the removed currency sink as a complete explanation; agricultural labor earnings, ownership of output, processing margins, and households' ability to use raw food need further work and measurement.

Procedural site 0 first records zero-energy residents at the day-16 sample and finishes with 11/22. Site 3 has none at the samples through day 24, then one at day 28 and three at day 30. Their different trajectories demonstrate that the same mechanics can produce different outcomes; one seed and two procedural sites do not establish broad viability or a proven resource-to-outcome relationship. Even the better site is deteriorating by the end of this observation window.

The system can now connect household support, paid procurement, interrupted inputs, labor capacity, resource depletion, and local knowledge to food access. Recovery from a stores-only shock is also possible when ripe fields and productive labor remain. However, civic/service income, sustained demand for some crafts, and household preparation of raw grain are still incomplete. Those gaps prevent treating all measured deprivation as a credible completed-economy failure. The next coherent economic work must address sustained earned income and usable household food before introducing permanent deprivation consequences; changing a survival target or adding unconditional transfers would not establish that support.

These generated artifacts are ignored by Git; the tables above retain their final measurements in repository documentation.

### Final verification

- Build/typecheck: passed (`npm run build`, including `tsc --noEmit`).
- Normal regression: **691/691 tests across 64 files passed** (`npm test -- --maxWorkers=2`).
- Causal-society acceptance: **7/7 passed** (`npm run causal:accept -- --maxWorkers=2`).
- Adaptive-society acceptance: **8/9 passed; 1 failed** (`npm run adapt:accept -- --maxWorkers=2`). The existing check `6. the shortage eased without being cured` fails at `tests/adaptive-society-longrun.test.ts:132`: `report.acceptance.shortageEased` is empty. Production loss, downstream shortage, plausible autonomous responders, practice-based improvement, resumed production, causal tracing, and earned skills passed their checks. The daily downstream flour-stock observation did not demonstrate the required recovery. The failure is retained; its root cause has not been isolated, and no threshold, seed, duration, or assertion was changed to make it pass.
- Final long-run observation: all three 30-day runs completed on the verified runtime, with the outcomes above. Completing these runs is not a pass of the unmet survival target.

Targeted navigation, player embodiment, living economy, pathfinding-livelock, and crop lifecycle checks passed before the final suite. No runtime changes followed the final build and regression run. Save schema 20 intentionally requires a newly initialized world; older saves are rejected. No commit or push has been made.

This is an incomplete milestone checkpoint, not a successful survival acceptance. All final jobs have ended. The adaptive recovery failure and the measured affordability regression remain open alongside the economic limitations above; additional broad reruns of unchanged code would not resolve them.
