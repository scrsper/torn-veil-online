## What advances, and why

The full classification ladder remains **Normal, Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold,
Diamond, God, Astral King, Astral Being, John Smith**. Labels never win combat.
The existing multi-day embodied-practice route to Iron is unchanged. This adds an
explicitly authored **essence-transformation route**, applicable to any person
with the real learned sources and encounter evidence. It never manufactures days,
attributes, prior practice, legacy XP, health or an inventory copy.

| Next transformation | Distinct opposed victories | Demonstrated forms | Different essences learned | Spare essence units consumed | Force amplitude / reach aperture / mana reserve |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Iron | 12 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1.6 / 1.2 / 120 |
| Bronze | 80 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2.8 / 2 / 150 |
| Silver | 360 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 7 / 5 / 220 |
| Gold | 1,000 | 6 | 6 | 4 | 20 / 12 / 320 |

Each form must actually damage three distinct opponents through its learned source.
Victories require a real agreed opponent or actual aggressor and at least half of
that opponent's maximum health in personal damage. A recovered or second-bodied
opponent cannot grant another victory. Surrender/withdrawal may resolve a trial but
does not award a personal victory. Empty casts and time spent waiting grant nothing.

Gold additionally requires a mean encounter-control score of at least 0.97. Each
victory pins incoming attack receipts; its score is `max(0, 1 - received damage /
maximum health)`. Healing cannot erase that encounter cost. This is an authored
eligibility rule, **not evidence that 1–5% of human climbers will become Gold**.
The prototype retains at most 2,048 distinct victory proofs and 64 target bodies
per learned form. A low lifetime mean at that ceiling can prevent further Gold
eligibility on this route. Distinct weak opponents can still contribute; the
current gate does not measure challenge relative to the caster's capacity.
Those are explicit progression-design limits to resolve before treating this as
a production mastery system, with receipt-preserving rules and new exploit tests.

Integration is an explicit, bound, cancellable queued command. It requires an
awake, recovered, nourished body outside close trial contact. It consumes finite
essence stock and energy/hydration, adds lasting fatigue, and emits the canonical
advancement with the source, demonstration, victory and material provenance.
The larger reserve is capacity, not an immediate mana refill. Injuries remain.

Physical components change accepted cast force, reach, radius and reserve. At
zero Veilcraft, Ember changes from 16 damage / 7 m to Silver's 112 / 35 m or Gold's
320 / 84 m before real protection, sight, geometry, timing and resource checks.
An ordinary person can use an actual equipped focus without gaining a rank.
Focus and embodied components take componentwise maxima; they do not multiply.

These are provisional classifications of the implemented arcane route. They do
not yet prove the Constitution's regional/national strategic meanings in a working
geopolitical simulation. Higher stages remain distinct extension points. Pure
martial progression beyond the existing Iron route is not implemented here.

## Summit and difficulty

Floor 100 can be physically cleared without receiving a rank. Summit recognition
requires already-earned Silver or higher. A climber who skipped source practice
can therefore clear the trials yet remain in an open run. The HUD explains this;
there is no fabricated reward or floor 101. Further martial routes and a voluntary
training/rematch interface are needed to make that goal accessible to every build.
Completion is terminal in the present run model: the earned record survives return
and portal travel, but completed trial rooms cannot currently be revisited through
a new entry. The physical city Tower/atrium remains accessible.

Wardens remain ordinary perceiving, fearful, tired people. Beginning at floor 4
they carry real weapons; floor 5 adds learned casts, floor 8 armor, floor 10 heavy
weapons, and later floors stronger finite focus equipment and varied kernels.
Ordinary action costs, sight, interruptions, damage, injury and friendly fire apply.
Floor population is capped at 32. Nothing copies the legacy attack-token system.
The final saved histories retain 293 and 283 actual NPC cast receipts respectively,
spanning all six authored warden kernels. Those link to 191 and 193 positive-damage
arcane hits on the player. These are canonical saved-receipt observations from the
accelerated runs, not rendered observations or complete tallies after compaction.
Encounter difficulty is not proven to increase on every floor. Source breakthroughs
produce large physical power jumps, and room sightlines and enemy responses vary.
Equipment escalation is implemented; smooth human pacing remains calibration work.

The finite reward rotation supplies a learning dose and an integration dose for
essences, plus two food portions. Each newly created floor has an eight-litre
entrance water intake, consumed through the shared player/NPC water path in
quarter-litre units. Depletion survives reload; no transition restores these stocks.
The existing real rest/wake and paid Mend actions provide recovery, without
removing injuries. Earlier saved floors are not retroactively restocked.

Calibration exposed a multi-party resolution defect: a warden could surrender to
another warden, or a later unrelated conflict could hide a genuine downing. Floor
retirement now recognizes the actual terminal receipt among trial participants.
Personal advancement continues to use its separate contribution requirements.

## Simulation and persistence

Completed remote floors retain every identity, body, injury, item and receipt.
Their detailed thought, perception, movement, posture, exertion, recoil and arcana
work is excluded. When the player returns to the city, current-floor residents
also leave detailed processing. One remote floor, at most 32 wardens, receives
coarse needs/recovery per world minute. Saved timestamps account for elapsed time;
coarse updates cannot invent practice or offscreen combat. Returning resumes the
same residents. Generated interior plans remain a disposable cache of at most 12.
The final repair invalidates the residency cache when canonical people/body index
arrays are rebuilt, including replacements with the same active count. A focused
regression reproduced the stale-body selection before this repair and passes after it.

This is a floor-residency budget, not constant-cost global simulation. Other
society/Chronicle/spatial-index work and the saved history still grow with population
and events. A production megacity needs broader locality budgets and persistence
compaction; these results do not establish millions of simulated residents.

Save schema 31 reads 24–31. Growth proofs, accepted casts, transformation components,
consumed amounts and material provenance are validated; inconsistent saves fail
closed. Evidence is pinned through event compaction. Legacy expedition rewards stay
separate. Installed Babylon assets/action adapters and archived Unreal are retained.
The separate Tower-polish worktree and its denied QA action were not touched.
