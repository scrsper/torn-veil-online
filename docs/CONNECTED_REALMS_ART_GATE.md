# Representative scene art gate: Threshold Square

**Status: NOT PASSED.** The user's direction is for the whole Veil to look exceptionally beautiful.
These functional procedural blocks do not meet that bar. Test success, addressable world size and a selected
skyline angle are not visual acceptance. Do not expand the quantity of this visual treatment as if it were final.

The representative scene is Aether Reach's Threshold Square at seed 918271: approach the actual Tower from
street level, inspect its entrance/material joints, enter its atrium, return past the portal and look down the
adjoining street. Cinderveil uses the same structural checks with a distinct fantasy/steampunk material and roof
grammar. This is one coherent scene review, not an assortment of unrelated concept images.

## Actual evidence and defects

The October 8 follow-up improves the representative Tower facades, window/door construction, entry
lighting and flush plaza detail in both realms. Actual before/after full frames, near views, checks and
cost observations are in [ART_OCT8_RESULTS.md](evidence/connected-realms/ART_OCT8_RESULTS.md). Canonical
geometry and gameplay remain unchanged. This improves the local scene; it does **not** pass the overall
art gate. The table below records the baseline problems that motivated the pass; broader district
variety, street furnishing, atmosphere across time states and character quality remain unfinished.

The recorded journey and `docs/evidence/connected-realms/` contain unretouched full frames. Supplemental entrance
views are in that directory as `art-square.png` and `art-entrance.png` (the defect-revealing pass before the
aperture trim correction); the repeatable camera route uses only
normal navigation and keyboard input. `TV_REALMS_ART_REVIEW=1` with `scripts/web/realms-capture.mjs` captures it
from the Aether test world. These are detail-distance views, not crops or model substitutions.

| Area | Current judgment | Required representative-scene improvement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Architecture | The Tower and surrounding blocks still share too much rectangular facade grammar. A sign and extra height do not make a strong landmark. | Curated base/shaft/crown silhouette, credible supports and openings, distinct shop/industrial/domestic bays, coordinated facade depth and trim. |
| Materials | Flat pale walls, near-black strips, glowing rectangles and repeated paving dominate. Existing textures add surface detail without resolving the form/material hierarchy. | Coherent stone/metal/glass/plaster treatment, controlled reflectance and roughness, convincing joints/edge detail, consistent texel scale and authored variation. |
| Street and terrain | Long flat grid avenues, large bare surfaces and few meaningful objects make the world feel empty. | One authored plaza/street composition with terrain transitions, believable kerbs/drainage, entries, useful furniture, greenery and clear destinations; procedural constraints preserve those relationships. |
| Lighting | Readability improved from the first pass, but disabling batch shadow reception flattened contact and depth. | Calibrated sunlight/sky/contact shadows, restrained emissive windows/signs, exposure and haze that preserve material separation at street distance. Test multiple time states. |
| Interior | The atrium is physically valid but sparse and generic. | Authored architectural modules, material transitions, meaningful side-room furnishing and motivated light, preserving the navigable aisle and true doorway. |
| Characters | Current shared bodies are rejected placeholders. | Adopt the dedicated character owner's inspected geometry/face/anatomy/hands/clothing output through the existing shared asset contract. No competing character-authoring fork here. |
| Streaming | The bounded window works, but cold uploads visibly stall. | A real per-frame work budget, asynchronous generation/upload and material warmup, verified during an ordinary walk through the representative scene. |

The close-up review found facade windows floating across the Tower aperture and a low cornice crossing its
opening. The final candidate excludes those windows and lower front bands. The combined browser approach capture in `evidence/connected-realms/combined/world-before-ascent.png` verifies the corrected opening in the merged build. This is a concrete aperture fix,
not a claim that the broader architecture or lighting has become beautiful. The earlier sky clipping and
floating roof tips were also fixed after inspecting actual rendered frames.

## Gate before repeating the art treatment

1. Establish a cohesive architectural/material palette for the whole square, including both exterior and atrium.
   Use the coordinator's verified Gigacity observations for layered detail and atmosphere; do not copy its code,
   unlicensed assets or assume its shader interiors are navigable rooms.
2. Review the ordinary gameplay approach and entrance close-up, the adjoining street, the interior and portal
   return in full frames. Include awkward joints and near views. Record unresolved defects openly.
3. Confirm the shared canonical collision/opening/navigation descriptions still agree with what the player sees.
   General remodelling and decorative prop colliders must be completed before advertising those interactions.
4. Measure representative traversal/loading with the intended hardware and disclose recording/background load.
   Avoid expensive GPU benchmarking while the character author is generating or inspecting assets.
5. Only after this scene satisfies the visual bar should its curated modules and variation rules be applied to
   larger districts. Preserve seed/building identity and migration versions when physical layouts change.

No money, external assets of uncertain license, reference uploads, publishing, or additional persistent access
are implied by the quality direction. The finished-world ambition remains intact; this branch delivers the
playable and expandable simulation/streaming foundation, with the art gate explicitly unfinished.
