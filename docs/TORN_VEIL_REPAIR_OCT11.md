## Growth and difficulty

Source transformation now requires 48 / 180 / 600 / 1280 distinct opposed victories for Iron / Bronze / Silver / Gold, plus 1 / 2 / 3 / 6 demonstrated kernels and learned essences, finite material consumption, recovery and break-contact requirements. A kernel must be applied to three distinct opponents. Gold also requires at least 97% sustained encounter control from pinned received-damage receipts. Its provisional policy pause is removed under the user's repair authorization. Earned ranks and saved source receipts are retained.

No floor counter or elapsed-time award promotes the body. Capacity changes physical force, reach and reserve; labels do not win combat. The summit requires independently earned Silver capacity. Every new floor increases actual warden armor, with later weapon, caster and focus kits and more opponents. These are authored encounter parameters, not a hidden rank comparison. New advancement events pin growth policy 2; old events validate against their retained historical rules. A genuine legacy Iron fixture generated with the previous executable verifies that stricter future gates do not invalidate an earned rank.

These thresholds are policy parameters. The several-hour climb and rare Gold target remain **unmeasured in fresh human play**. Fast threshold fixtures use controlled opponents and direct finishing hits; they prove provenance, costs, save validation and attainable Gold, not strategy, pacing or population rarity. The Observatory exposes real floor-clear elapsed seconds and stored causes, including time away, so those are not mislabeled as active-play duration.
